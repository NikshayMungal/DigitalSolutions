The True Cost of Employment

Your real selling value goes beyond basic gross salaries. Because employers also cover UIF, leave days, sick leave, equipment overhead, and management time, the true cost of a R14,000 clerk is closer to R16,000 to R17,000. This makes your 50% retainer structure drop to roughly 40% of the true cost, making your automated solution an effortless business decision.

Capacity & Volume Multiplier

Automation percentage does not mean cutting your team to zero. Even if 70% of a role is automated, the business still relies on human oversight for the remaining 30%. Instead of replacing staff, frame it precisely: "You don’t need to hire a second clerk as you scale" or "Your existing clerk effortlessly handles twice the volume."
